# Home by Five Website Architecture

## Primary Journey

The site moves a multi-location owner through five questions:

1. Why is this relevant to my business?
2. What questions will it answer?
3. How does the answer become a decision?
4. Which level of support do I need?
5. What happens when I choose a plan?

The primary conversion is plan selection followed by a fit conversation. The interactive Atlas Home Services dashboard is product proof, not the homepage itself.

## URL Map

| Page | URL | Job | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage | `/` | Establish the problem, promise, method, proof, and plans | See the command center |
| Product | `/product/` | Explain the Numbers → Decisions → Strategy → Human Touch system | Explore the live demo |
| Who It Is For | `/who-its-for/` | Help franchise and multi-location buyers recognize their use cases | Find the right plan |
| Pricing | `/pricing/` | Make the three founding subscriptions easy to compare | Choose a plan |
| Insights | `/insights/` | Show the research thesis and integrity rules without publishing unvalidated claims | Discuss your network |
| Why Home by Five | `/about/` | Connect the brand mission to better operating decisions | See how it works |
| Interactive Demo | `/demo/` | Demonstrate the product with synthetic data | Compare plans |
| Request a Plan | `/start/` | Select a plan and prepare an onboarding request | Submit once lead delivery is connected |

## Header Navigation

Product · Who It Is For · Pricing · Insights · Why Home by Five · See the Demo

The header remains intentionally flat. Every critical page is one click from every other page. The logo returns to the homepage.

## Homepage Argument

1. Outcome: know where the next marketing dollar and hour should go.
2. Audience: multi-location service providers and franchise owners.
3. Problem: more reports, fewer answers, and unclear ownership of local performance.
4. Product logic: Numbers → Decisions → Strategy → Human Touch.
5. Product proof: interactive Atlas Home Services demo.
6. Use cases: market, marketing, conversion, attribution, and capacity diagnosis.
7. Offer: three transparent founding subscriptions.
8. Mission: finish the decision and get home earlier.
9. Conversion: choose a plan and request a fit conversation.

## Launch Boundaries

- No invented customers, testimonials, logos, or outcome claims.
- The Local Control Gap is labeled research in progress until the methodology validates a conclusion.
- The subscription prices are shown exactly as provided, but integration scope, location limits, billing, and terms remain onboarding decisions.
- The request form is a front-end demo until a booking link, CRM endpoint, or destination email is provided.
- The demo remains synthetic and explicitly labeled.
