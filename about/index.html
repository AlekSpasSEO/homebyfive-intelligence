The mission

The name is the operating principle

Clarity should give time back.

Multi-location growth creates more markets, channels, systems, vendors, and opinions. Without a decision layer, every question becomes a report request, every report creates a meeting, and every meeting creates another analysis. Home by Five is designed to close that loop.

What we believe

Better decisions require both rigor and restraint.

The product should help leaders act—not manufacture certainty the evidence cannot support.

01

Numbers should reconcile to the business

Marketing activity matters when it connects to demand, leads, bookings, revenue, cost, and capacity. Unresolved differences remain visible.

02

A dashboard is unfinished without a decision

Leadership does not need another collection of charts. It needs a ranked answer, the reason behind it, and a clear next move.

03

Central control and local freedom can coexist

HQ should own measurement, standards, architecture, attribution, and allocation. Local operators should have room for offers, community, partnerships, and market-specific opportunities inside those guardrails.

04

Automation follows validated work

Agents and workflows should emerge from repeated decisions with known inputs, outputs, QA risks, and human review—not from an imagined process.

05

The evidence must be allowed to disagree

We do not choose the headline number first, confuse correlation with causation, or turn an illustrative range into a guarantee.

The useful outcome

Fewer unresolved loops at the end of the day.

Home by Five should leave the team with an answer that fits on one page.

1

What happened?

A consistent number across the network, locations, channels, and reporting windows.

2

Why might it have happened?

Observed evidence, transparent rules, and uncertainty separated from assumptions.

3

What should we do?

A short, ranked decision queue instead of a broad list of possible marketing tactics.

4

Who owns it?

A clear handoff to leadership, strategy, operations, the location, or a human specialist.

5

How will we know?

A measurement rule decided before the next dollar or hour is committed.

5

Can we finish now?

The decision is recorded, the next review is clear, and the team can stop circling the same question.
