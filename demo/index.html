How the numbers work

Every figure, score and finding in this 24-location network is synthetic. This is a product demo, not an assessment of an actual business.

Complete, comparable windows

Month uses a complete calendar month; Quarter uses a complete calendar quarter; 6 months uses Jan–Jun or Jul–Dec. The latest complete windows in the demo are August 2026, Q2 2026 and Jan–Jun 2026. Previous compares the immediately preceding window of equal length. Year over year shifts the same window back 12 months. Totals sum monthly records; ratios are calculated from totals, not averages of percentages.

Revenue-based ROI, not profit

ROI = (Revenue − Marketing spend) ÷ Marketing spend × 100. Marketing spend includes advertising plus allocated marketing and management costs. Delivery costs, cost of goods, overhead and taxes are excluded, so this is not profit ROI or a causal measure of marketing lift. A 100% ROI here means $2 of recorded revenue for every $1 of marketing cost.

Channel views

All channels includes all recorded revenue. Individual channels include revenue linked to that source in the synthetic model. Unresolved sources remain separate rather than being assigned to Direct & Referral. Channel matching does not establish causation. Channel ROI is withheld when source coverage in the selected location scope is below 50%. Paid combines Google Ads and Social Ads; Social combines Social Ads and Community. These groups overlap and must not be summed together.

Monthly opportunity

This is an illustrative range of additional monthly revenue, not a forecast, profit estimate or selected-period total. It uses the final month of the reporting window. For each eligible growth market: incremental qualified lead headroom × 40–70% capture × booking conversion × average job value, capped by spare delivery capacity. The lower and upper location scenarios are summed across distinct fictional territories. Channel views allocate the same opportunity using modeled demand shares; they do not create extra opportunity. The score gap alone never determines dollars.

Potential and presence

Potential = 50% local service demand + 30% serviceable households + 20% competitive headroom. Presence = 45% search-result visibility + 35% Maps visibility + 20% brand recognition. All components are fictional normalized 0–100 inputs; these indices are not percentages, validated market sizes or market share. Detailed location views show each component.

Findings and charts

Findings check source quality first, then booking conversion, then market scores. Open each finding to see the exact rule and observations. Scores use the latest month; booking conversion and source coverage use the selected window. Opportunity uses the final month's inputs. Monthly charts use illustrative daily allocations that sum exactly to the monthly totals. Comparison days align by day number, with missing days left blank; quarter and half-year charts align corresponding months.
