The diagnostic layer

Do not prescribe more marketing until you know the problem.

Home by Five is designed to distinguish five situations that look similar in a topline report but require very different decisions.

01

Market problem

The serviceable opportunity is smaller than the raw revenue target assumes.

02

Visibility problem

Demand exists, but the location is not easy enough to find.

03

Conversion problem

Leads arrive, but calls, forms, offers, or follow-up leak bookings.

04

Data problem

The source match is too weak to judge channel economics honestly.

05

Capacity problem

More demand could damage close rate, service quality, or reputation.
