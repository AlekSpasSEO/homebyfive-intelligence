Questions before choosing

Clear scope starts with a fit conversation.

Are the prices per location?

The three prices are shown exactly as the current founding subscription amounts. The final commercial model—flat fee, included location count, or per-location scaling—has not yet been validated. We confirm the location scope before activation rather than hiding it in fine print.

What data does Home by Five need?

The useful minimum depends on the decision. Revenue, marketing spend, leads or calls, bookings, and location structure create the foundation. Local visibility, reviews, capacity, finance, CRM, and source-quality inputs improve diagnosis. Missing inputs stay visible.

Is ROI profit-based?

Only when the required margin and cost data is available and the metric is labeled that way. The current demo shows revenue-based ROI: revenue minus marketing spend, divided by marketing spend. Delivery costs, overhead, taxes, and causal lift are not implied.

Can I change plans later?

The website is currently presenting founding packages, not completed billing terms. Plan changes, cancellation, annual billing, and refund rules must be confirmed in the service agreement before public checkout is enabled.

Does the $100 plan still give decisions?

Yes. Decisions are the core product, not an upgrade. Strategy adds an ordered growth plan, and Human Touch adds direct judgment and review.

Will this replace our agencies and systems?

Not necessarily. Home by Five is designed as the decision layer across them. It can reveal where a source, vendor, or workflow needs improvement without pretending one tool should perform every local marketing task.
