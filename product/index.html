How it works

From fragmented signals to a decision you can defend.

The workflow is intentionally simple for leaders, even when the underlying data is not.

01

Build the operating picture

Bring location, channel, revenue, conversion, source quality, local visibility, and capacity into a common structure. Missing data stays visible rather than being silently “fixed.”

02

Compare like with like

Use complete month, quarter, or six-month windows. Compare with the preceding equal-length period or year over year. Evaluate location performance in the context of its market.

03

Identify the constraint

Ask whether the issue is market size, visibility, conversion, source quality, operator execution, or capacity before recommending another dollar of acquisition.

04

Explain the next move

Each finding includes its rule, supporting observations, uncertainty, and a concrete next step. Strategy and human plans then turn the decision into an ordered program.

Decision guardrails

Clear answers without pretending the data is perfect.

Trust comes from knowing what a number can support—and what it cannot.

≠

Attribution is not causation

A source match helps organize revenue. It does not prove the channel created the sale.

%

ROI is defined visibly

Revenue-based ROI is separated from profit ROI, with cost coverage and missing inputs disclosed.

~

Opportunity uses ranges

Demand, capture, conversion, job value, and capacity produce a scenario—not a guaranteed forecast.

P

Scores have components

Market potential and local presence show their definitions and weights instead of hiding behind a number.

!

Weak data blocks confidence

When source coverage is too low, channel ROI is withheld and data reconciliation becomes the action.

H

Humans own consequential judgment

The system accelerates analysis. Leaders and strategists decide how context changes the action.
