Who it is for

Every location is different. The decision system should still be consistent.

Home by Five is for leaders responsible for growth across multiple service locations—whether those locations are corporate-owned, franchised, or a mix of both.

Franchise systems

Keep one standard without flattening local reality.

  • Make marketing-fund performance easier to explain
  • Compare locations after accounting for market opportunity
  • See where local execution breaks national brand strength
  • Give franchisees clear priorities inside shared guardrails
  • Separate operator, marketing, and market problems
Multi-location service providers

Allocate attention and money across the portfolio.

  • See which branches can absorb more demand
  • Find paid, organic, social, community, and AI-search gaps
  • Connect calls and leads to bookings and revenue
  • Protect high-performing markets before they slip
  • Stop letting raw revenue hide weak unit economics
The people in the room

One answer, viewed from four responsibilities.

The same operating picture helps each stakeholder make a different part of the decision.

O

Owner or CEO

Where is the network creating economic value, and where should capital or leadership attention move next?

M

Marketing leader

Which channel and local visibility opportunities deserve a test—and which locations need conversion help first?

P

Operations leader

Where are response, booking, delivery capacity, or local execution limiting the return on demand?

F

Finance leader

How much revenue is reconciled, what costs are included, and where is the evidence too weak for confidence?

L

Location operator

What is the one local action that matters now, and how does it connect to booked work rather than marketing activity?

S

Strategic partner

What should be tested, sequenced, governed, or left alone across the network?

Good fit

You have a recurring cross-location decision.

The product becomes more valuable when leadership needs to compare competing uses of money and attention.

Home by Five may fit when…

  • You operate or support several local service markets
  • Marketing, sales, operations, and finance see different numbers
  • Location performance varies for reasons that raw revenue does not explain
  • You need to decide where incremental budget should go
  • You want central intelligence with controlled local freedom

It is probably not the right fit when…

  • You operate one location with no portfolio allocation problem
  • You want guaranteed revenue or a black-box prediction
  • You want a single channel vendor to grade only its own work
  • You are unwilling to expose missing or inconsistent data
  • You only need a static monthly report
